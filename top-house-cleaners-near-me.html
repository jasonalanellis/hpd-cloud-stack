Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What's the difference between "cleaning" and "sanitizing"?

A: "Cleaning" removes visible dirt, dust, and debris from surfaces. "Sanitizing" reduces the number of microorganisms on a surface to a safe level, as determined by public health standards, but does not necessarily kill all germs. True professionals aim for a sanitization-first approach.

Q: How often should I schedule professional house cleaning in San Diego?

A: This depends on your lifestyle, home size, and presence of pets or allergies. For most San Diego homeowners, bi-weekly service is ideal for maintaining a high level of cleanliness. Monthly service can work for smaller homes or those with less activity, while weekly service offers the highest standard of continuous care.

Q: Should I provide cleaning supplies, or do top house cleaners near me bring their own?

A: Professional cleaning services always bring their own, professional-grade equipment and solutions. This ensures they have the right tools for every task and maintain control over the quality and effectiveness of their products. If a service asks you to provide supplies, it's a red flag.

Q: What insurance should a professional cleaning service carry?

A: At a minimum, they should carry General Liability insurance (typically $1 million per occurrence) to protect against property damage. If they have employees, Workers' Compensation insurance is also critical to protect you from liability in case of employee injury on your property. Always request current Certificates of Insurance directly from their insurer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify if trusted HVAC service providers in my city today carry valid insurance?

Request a Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming you as the specific certificate holder. Call the insurance carrier's phone number listed directly on the document to confirm active policy status, policy limits, and current Workers' Compensation enrollment.

Why is an ACCA Manual J load calculation necessary for system replacement?

An ACCA Manual J calculation establishes the precise cooling and heating load of your home by factoring in insulation density, duct location, orientation, and window performance. Skipping this calculation usually leads to oversized equipment, high utility costs, and uneven room temperatures.

What static pressure reading indicates a properly designed duct system?

Total external static pressure (TESP) on a typical residential air handler should generally not exceed 0.5 inches of water column (in. w.c.). Static pressure readings exceeding 0.8 in. w.c. restrict system airflow, decrease operating efficiency, and lead to premature mechanical failure.

What is the practical difference between SEER and SEER2 ratings?

SEER2 relies on the M1 testing standard established by the Department of Energy, which tests equipment under higher external static pressure to mimic real-world duct conditions. A SEER2 rating provides a much more accurate projection of actual seasonal power consumption than older SEER metrics.
